Public-health interpretation

CFR

Deaths ÷ cases for each reported outbreak event. It is not a measure of population mortality and is sensitive to case ascertainment and reporting delays.

Cumulative burden

Adds event-level totals across the selected historical series. Cross-year events are counted once.

Contribution share

Shows how much each event contributes to the total recorded case burden in this dataset.
