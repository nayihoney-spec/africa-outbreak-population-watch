Research & therapies

Evidence status matters: a therapy effective for Zaire ebolavirus cannot automatically be assumed effective for Bundibugyo virus.

Established for Zaire ebolavirus

Monoclonal antibody therapies

Inmazeb (REGN-EB3) and Ebanga (ansuvimab) are established treatments for Zaire ebolavirus disease. Species-specific evidence and regulatory labeling must be checked before extrapolation.

Current 2026 research context

Bundibugyo virus candidates

WHO’s 2026 situation page states there is no vaccine or specific treatment for the Bundibugyo species involved in the current outbreak, while promising candidates are being tested.
