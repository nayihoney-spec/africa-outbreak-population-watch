Major milestones

A concise public-education timeline grounded in WHO, CDC and peer-reviewed sources.

Home
1976

First recognized outbreaks

Simultaneous outbreaks occurred in what are now South Sudan and the Democratic Republic of the Congo; the virus was named after the Ebola River.

2014–2016

West Africa epidemic

The largest Ebola epidemic on record transformed global preparedness, vaccine development and clinical research.

2018–2020

Eastern DRC outbreak

A major Zaire ebolavirus outbreak in North Kivu, Ituri and South Kivu occurred amid insecurity and population movement.

2019–2020

Vaccines and therapeutics mature

Regulatory approvals and randomized-trial evidence changed prevention and treatment for Zaire ebolavirus disease.

2026

Bundibugyo virus disease outbreak

The current DRC outbreak is caused by Bundibugyo virus. WHO notes there is currently no licensed vaccine or specific treatment for this species; candidate work is ongoing.
