EBOLA MILESTONES

From discovery to today

A concise public-health timeline of major Ebola milestones, outbreaks, vaccines and therapeutics.

Home

1976 · First recognized Ebola disease outbreaks

Outbreaks in what is now the Democratic Republic of the Congo and in Sudan led to the first recognition of Ebola disease. The DRC outbreak near Yambuku reported 318 cases and 280 deaths.

2014–2016 · West Africa epidemic

The largest Ebola epidemic on record, with more than 28,600 cases reported across Guinea, Liberia and Sierra Leone, transformed global outbreak preparedness and accelerated vaccine and therapeutic development.

2018–2020 · Eastern DRC

The North Kivu / Ituri / South Kivu outbreak recorded 3,470 cases and 2,287 deaths. It became a major setting for ring vaccination and randomized therapeutic evaluation.

2019 · Ervebo vaccine milestone

Ervebo became the first FDA-approved vaccine for prevention of Ebola virus disease caused by Ebola virus (formerly Zaire ebolavirus). WHO now prequalifies and recommends it for EBOV outbreak response.

2020 · First approved Ebola therapeutics

Inmazeb and Ebanga were approved in the United States for Ebola virus disease caused by Orthoebolavirus zairense, following evidence including the PALM trial.

2026 · Bundibugyo virus outbreak and research response

The current DRC outbreak is caused by Bundibugyo virus. WHO states that there is no licensed BDBV-specific vaccine and that evidence is insufficient to establish human protection from Ervebo; use is recommended within research protocols. A PARTNERS therapeutic trial is enrolling patients.
